Good to know

Quick answers

How much space do the booths need?

The mirror booth works best with about 8 × 8 feet of level floor space; the 360 booth needs roughly 10 × 10 feet including a clear safety perimeter. Standard ceilings are fine.

Do you travel outside Halifax and the South Shore?

Yes — we serve communities across Nova Scotia. A travel fee may apply by distance, and Atlantic Canada events are quoted separately.

Do guests get prints or digital copies?

The Mirror Photo Booth prints photos on site and shares digital copies. The 360 Video Booth delivers short, music-ready video clips straight to guests' phones.

Are both booths available to book?

Yes — the Mirror Photo Booth and the 360 Video Booth are both available for events across Nova Scotia. Get a quote and we'll confirm availability for your date.
