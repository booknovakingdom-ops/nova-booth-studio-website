The Experience

What a mirror photo booth is

Picture a full-length mirror that talks back. Guests walk up, the glass comes alive with touch animations that guide them through the shot, and a few poses later a photo — designed to match the event — is ready to print and share.

It's the format made for dressed-up moments: outfits appear head to toe, groups fit naturally in the frame, and the experience itself becomes part of the entertainment.

What to expect

What’s included

A polished, hosted experience built to give guests a keepsake they can print, share and remember.

  • Interactive full-length mirror experience — touch animations that guide every guest
  • Custom photo template options — designed to match your event, from wedding monograms to brand marks
  • Prints and digital sharing — memories can travel home in hand and by phone
  • An attendant with the booth — hosted the way our 360 experience is hosted today
  • Setup and takedown handled by us — arranged around your venue and schedule

FAQ

Mirror booth questions

When can I book the mirror photo booth?

Yes. The Mirror Photo Booth is available now. Send us your event date, venue and city and we'll confirm availability.

Can I plan a future event around it?

Yes. Tell us your event date and venue through the quote form and we’ll confirm availability for the Mirror Photo Booth.

Will templates be customizable?

Yes. Custom photo template design can match your names, colours, monogram or brand, just like custom overlays for our 360 booth.

How much space will it need?

Plan for about 8 × 8 feet of level floor space with a standard 120V outlet nearby. Standard ceiling heights are fine.
