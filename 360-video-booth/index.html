The Experience

Ten seconds on the platform. A clip they keep forever.

The 360 booth is the moment your dance floor has been waiting for. Guests step onto the platform, the camera makes a smooth orbit, and slow motion does the rest — endlessly rewatchable, made to be shared.

Each clip is finished with a custom overlay designed for your event and delivered digitally on the spot, so it's in group chats and stories while the song is still playing.

Safety

Fun, with a spotter

The platform moves, the camera swings, and the drinks stay elsewhere. A few simple rules keep every spin safe:

  • Guests must follow the operator's instructions.
  • No food, drinks or confetti are permitted on the platform.
  • Minors require appropriate adult supervision.
  • Unsafe or intoxicated guests may be refused.
  • Service may pause when conditions are unsafe.

FAQ

360 booth questions

How do guests get their videos?

Instantly and digitally — clips are shared straight to guests' phones at the booth, finished with your custom overlay and ready to post.

How many people fit on the platform?

It depends on the group — your operator will guide each spin so everyone fits safely and the clip looks its best. Solo spins and small groups both work beautifully.

Can the overlay match our wedding or brand?

Yes — a custom video overlay is part of every booking. Names, dates, monograms, logos or brand guidelines, approved by you before the event.

What does it cost?

Every event is different — date, duration, travel and design all play a part — so we quote each one individually. Get a quote and we'll reply with a clear, itemized number for your date.
