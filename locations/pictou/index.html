Local FAQ

Pictou questions

Can you set up during busy festival weeks?

Yes, with earlier booking — carnival-season weekends fill our calendar the way they fill your waterfront. Organizers should also confirm our floor spot early so the crowd flows around it.

Do waterfront venues cause problems for the booth?

Only the wind, and only sometimes. A corner inside or on the sheltered side keeps clips crisp; if conditions ever become unsafe, service pauses until they aren't.
