Local FAQ

Annapolis Royal questions

Will the booth damage or crowd a heritage venue?

No — the platform is freestanding, needs no fixings, and we plan its footprint with your venue in advance. We treat old rooms with the respect they've earned.

Can you set up in the gardens?

For outdoor garden events we need a level, dry, covered spot with power in reach — a marquee works beautifully. Weather backup gets agreed with you beforehand, and service may pause if conditions turn unsafe.
