Local FAQ

Mahone Bay questions

Our venue is small — will the booth fit?

Quite possibly. The 360 platform needs about 10 × 10 feet including its safety perimeter. Send us your room's dimensions and we'll tell you honestly whether it works, and where it works best.

Should we book early for festival weekends?

Yes — Mahone Bay's festival dates concentrate a lot of events into a few weekends, and our calendar reflects that. The earlier you enquire, the more likely your first-choice date is open.
