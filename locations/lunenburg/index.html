Local FAQ

Lunenburg questions

Can the booth get into Lunenburg's historic venues?

Usually, yes — we plan around stairs, doorways and older buildings all the time. Share the venue name and any access quirks when you enquire and we'll confirm the plan before your date.

We're planning a tented reception — will the 360 booth work?

Yes, provided the tent gives us a level, dry footprint of about 10 × 10 feet and power within reach. Harbour wind is worth planning for; a sidewall near the booth keeps everything polished.
