Local FAQ

Wolfville questions

Will the booth work at an outdoor vineyard reception?

Yes, with a level, dry, covered spot about 10 × 10 feet and power within reach — a marquee corner or event-barn doorway is ideal. Vineyard evenings cool off fast; indoors-adjacent placement keeps the line long all night.

Do you host university events?

Yes — formals and society events are a great fit. An operator supervises the platform for the entire booking, and appropriate adult supervision is required for any minors.
