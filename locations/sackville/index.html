Local FAQ

Sackville questions

Do you work school events in Sackville?

Yes — proms, formals and school celebrations are a natural fit. An operator hosts the platform for the entire booking, minors require appropriate adult supervision, and we're happy to coordinate expectations with staff or organizers in advance.

What does a community centre need to provide?

About 10 × 10 feet of level floor away from the main traffic path and one standard 120V outlet. We handle delivery, setup and takedown outside your booked hours.
